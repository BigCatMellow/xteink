# Ink Runes — physical X4 Pro acceptance

1. Confirm XTOS Licorice and install ink-runes.xtapp through All Files.
2. Check the 480×800 portrait board, letters, question-mark notebook and PREV/UNDO/RESET/NEXT buttons all fit.
3. Tap M → I → R along adjoining squares on puzzle 1. Each tap makes just one still redraw; the valid rune covers the three letters with solid black cells.
4. Open ?, confirm MIR is learned, and confirm later rune powers are not shown before discovery.
5. Test UNDO after a successful rune and RESET after a partial trace. Previously unlocked levels should remain available.
6. Close and reopen: covered tiles and discovered rune powers must persist.
7. On level 5, verify VAS covers its path and neighbors of the final cell, not diagonal cells.
8. On level 9, verify DOK covers the entire last-cell column, not its row.
9. Test an invalid word or a nonadjacent selection: no cells are permanently covered.
10. Use a mistaken first move on a harder puzzle; check UNDO or RESET recovers it.
11. Verify 6×6 boards have readable letters and touch targets.
12. Use physical page buttons; they should never select letters.
13. Finish level 20, reboot, and confirm saved completion.
14. Leave idle: no animated states, periodic redraws or sound.

Hardware validation is pending until these checks pass. Record firmware build and any failed steps.
