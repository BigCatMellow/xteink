# Island Links — real X4 Pro acceptance

1. Install `island-links.xtapp` from All Files and open it in Ink App.
2. Confirm the puzzle title, islands and bottom controls fit in portrait 480×800.
3. Tap a potential link between two circles; a single line appears with **one** static redraw. Tap it again: two parallel lines. Third tap: empty.
4. Tap the center of a numbered island. It must not change a bridge.
5. Confirm no bridge is drawn diagonally or through another island.
6. Verify crossing bridges prevent success even when numbered totals match.
7. Place bridges until a valid solution: numbers match, no crossing, all islands connected, and NEXT unlocks.
8. Test UNDO and RESET; then leave the app, reopen, and confirm progress and undo history persist.
9. Check touch targeting at long bridges and at crossings between potential paths. Note any ambiguities.
10. Verify page buttons only navigate unlocked levels; they never toggle a link.
11. Complete all 12 stages; final level should show DONE, with saved completion after restart.
12. Check there are no moving elements, transitions, background redraws or grey patterns.

CI covers the geometric rules and unique solutions; actual physical device tests remain required.
