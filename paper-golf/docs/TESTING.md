# Physical X4 Pro acceptance checklist

CI is not a substitute for testing on a physical device.

1. Confirm Settings > About identifies XTOS Licorice on an X4 Pro.
2. Copy the downloaded XTApp through USB mode; install from All Files; launch from Ink App.
3. Confirm screen fits 480x800, with no crash or unreadable controls. Verify a dotted paper grid, continuous stepped fairway, wave-filled water clusters, dotted sand clusters, triangular trees, an outlined ball, and a small flag. No colors or large black filled regions.
4. Select PUTT 1, CHIP 2, DRIVE 4 and confirm each gives only exact-distance landing rings.
5. Tap a tree, water, and an unringed space: the ball should not move or spend a stroke.
6. Tap a ringed space: one stroke, new ball position, and you can choose another club.
7. Check restrictions: sand allows only PUTT, rough prevents DRIVE, water can be crossed but not landed on, trees block the line.
8. Reach the flag, then NEXT HOLE: the score and computed minimum TARGET update.
9. Leave and reopen mid-game: ball, chosen club, terrain and scorecard return exactly.
10. Finish nine holes: total strokes and target appear; new round works.
11. NEW -> Cancel preserves progress, NEW -> Yes resets it.
12. Check ghosting and physical-page-button input.

Report firmware build, step number and a photo of any device-side failure. Do not label the app hardware-verified until these pass.

The v0.3 update resets old dice-based saves on launch; confirm this migration is safe and produces a valid club-selection game.
